Air-con window panel

A clear acrylic panel that seals an awning window around a portable air-con hose.

Project
Client job
Year
2026
My role
Measure, design, cut and fit
Tools
Laser cutting, clear acrylic, rubber edge seal

Turning a duct-tape plan into a sealed panel the hose clicks into.

The brief

A portable air conditioner vents hot air through a hose. In this apartment the hose hung out of an open awning window, and the gaps around it let the hot air back in. The owner's plan was a sheet of plexiglass duct-taped over the gap, with a hole for the vent.

What I did

  • Went round the next day to measure the window and look at the vent adapter. The real opening was smaller than the first estimate, so the panel needed to be at least 750 × 585 mm.
  • Suggested a rubber edge seal sized to the window frame. It holds the panel in place and seals the edges, so there's no duct tape.
  • Laser cut the opening around the window kit's through-the-wall adapter, so the exhaust hose clicks in and stays put.
  • Supplied the clear acrylic, and returned the vent adapter with the finished panel.

The result

The panel sits in the frame on its seal, and the hose clips straight into the cutout. The owner's review says it better than I can.
