NeoNest smart NICU incubator

A neonatal incubator concept designed to ease nurse workload and help parents feel closer to their baby.

Project
Team university project
Year
2023
My role
Design team member
Tools
User research, 3D CAD, rendering

An incubator that tells the ward what it needs at a glance.

The brief

Our team was asked to redesign a neonatal incubator for a hospital NICU. An interview with a NICU nurse at a major Brisbane hospital pointed to three problems: heavy nurse workload, patchy communication between staff, and a stressful experience for parents.

What we did

  • Mapped the existing ward system from the interview, with mind maps, journey maps and stakeholder maps.
  • Designed a pod with an aluminium alloy frame, an injection-moulded polyethylene body and smart-glass windows that tint on demand.
  • Added coloured status lighting so staff can read the incubator's state from across the room.
  • Designed a front touch display for temperature, humidity, window tint and a camera view of the baby.
  • Gave the base powered height adjustment and a guided movement mode. It unlocks the wheels when a nurse holds the handle, and moves at a speed set by how hard they push.
  • Produced a dimensioned general arrangement and an exploded view.

The result

A rendered and dimensioned concept. The base adjusts from 800 mm to 1400 mm high, and the unit is 2381 mm long overall. The project was taken to concept stage for the course.
