E-scooter front wheel hub

The front hub for a team e-scooter, with bearing fits worked out properly and a full drawing pack.

Project
Team university project
Year
2026
My role
Hub CAD, tolerancing and all five drawings
Tools
SolidWorks, AS 1100, ISO 2768

Getting the bearing fits right on a hub with a fixed axle.

The brief

Our team was designing an e-scooter for a university engineering unit. My part was the front wheel hub: a complete assembly that carries a disc brake, runs on sealed bearings and bolts into a standard fork.

What I did

  • Modelled the hub in SolidWorks: a custom M12 axle, left and right wheel rims and a bearing spacer, built around off-the-shelf deep-groove bearings and a Magura MDR-C disc rotor.
  • Set the bearing fits. The outer rings turn with the wheel, so they take an interference fit (Ø28 N7). The inner rings sit on a fixed axle, so they take a slide fit (Ø12 h6) and are clamped by the nut, washer and spacer stack.
  • The first issue had k6 on the inner rings, which suits a rotating inner ring. I corrected it to h6.
  • Matched the rotor mount to Magura's 6 × M5 bolt pattern on a 128 mm PCD.
  • Called up surface finishes (Ra 1.6 on bearing seats, Ra 3.2 elsewhere), the M12 × 1.25 thread and the materials.
  • Produced a two-sheet assembly drawing with an itemised BOM, plus four part drawings, to AS 1100 and ISO 2768-mK.

The result

A complete, corrected drawing pack for the hub. The fit correction is the part I'd point to. It comes from understanding how the load moves through the bearings, not from the CAD software.
