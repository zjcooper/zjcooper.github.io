Caravan window replacement

A cracked caravan window replaced with a new acrylic pane, cut to the frame and sealed in.

Project
Client job
Year
2026
My role
Remove, measure, cut, fit and seal
Tools
3 mm acrylic, neutral-cure silicone

A cracked window on an old van, replaced so it looks right and seals properly.

The brief

The owner was restoring an older caravan, and one of its windows had cracked right across. She had already bought a 900 × 900 mm sheet of 3 mm acrylic. She needed someone to take the old pane out and fit the new one properly.

What I did

  • Took the cracked pane out of the top-hinged sash and cleared the old sealant from the frame.
  • Recorded the opening, including its rounded corners, and cut her acrylic sheet down to fit it.
  • Bedded the new pane in black neutral-cure silicone. She asked for black. I chose a neutral-cure product because some standard silicones can craze acrylic over time.
  • Did the job in two short visits: one to remove and measure, and one to fit.

The result

The new pane is in, with a neat black seal line all the way round, and the view through the window is clear again. The owner left a five-star review once it was done.
