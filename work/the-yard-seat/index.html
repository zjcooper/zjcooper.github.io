The Yard pegboard seat

A timber pegboard that wraps a concrete block in a campus courtyard, with a seat that moves between the holes.

Project
Team university project
Year
2024
My role
CAD, renders, CNC files and build
Tools
Fusion 360, KeyShot, CNC router, lathe

A seat that plugs into a pegboard, wherever you want it.

The brief

A furniture design course asked our team of four to design and build a piece for The Yard, a courtyard on campus. We chose one of the concrete blocks already there and designed a pegboard that wraps around it. A timber seat plugs into the holes on steel dowels, so people can set it up the way they want.

What we did

  • We tested a hanging table first. It was unstable, and we couldn't reach the bridge we planned to hang it from. So we moved to the concrete block.
  • We built a full-size test with timber dowels. It held 90 kg, but we needed a hammer to get the seat out. We changed to steel dowels in steel sleeves, and the test boards held 90 kg again.
  • I modelled the pegboard and the seat in Fusion 360. The design includes a 100 mm block that works as a backrest, footrest or divider.
  • I made the renders in KeyShot, including colour options and exploded views for the final presentation.
  • With a teammate, I prepared the CNC files. Then I checked the dimensions with the workshop technician before cutting.
  • With a teammate, I turned lead-in edges on all 92 steel sleeves and the three seat dowels on the lathe. Then I helped deburr, glue and finish the build.

The result

The team built the full pegboard and seat. We test-fitted the pegboard on the concrete block, then painted it blue and varnished the laminated timber seat.
