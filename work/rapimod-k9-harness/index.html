Rapimod K9 harness

A modular protective harness for police and military dogs, designed from handler research.

Project
University capstone
Year
2024
My role
Research, concept design, CAD and renders
Tools
User research, 3D CAD, rendering

Protection for working dogs that doesn't cook them in the heat.

The brief

Police and military dogs face two big risks on the job: heat stress, and stab or ballistic injury. The brief was a harness that protects them without overheating them, and that handlers can fit and release quickly in the field.

What I did

  • Interviewed a Queensland Police dog handler with more than nine years in the role.
  • Surveyed 13 K9 professionals in Australia and the US, and reviewed 18 police body-camera incidents.
  • Benchmarked five existing harnesses for protection, modularity, durability and fit.
  • Designed two configurations: soft Kevlar body protection, and a hard Dyneema tactical base harness.
  • Detailed the base harness with a 7.5 mm Dyneema HB50 plate, a cotton canvas outer, 15 mm 3D spacer foam for cooling, and metal buckles and snap fasteners. The handler asked for metal hardware.
  • Added a 360° camera, GPS tracking and a body-temperature sensor, with a companion phone app for location, the live camera and heat alerts.

The result

A modular harness system built around what handlers said mattered most. In the survey, 92.3% rated neck protection as important and 76.9% rated chest protection. The project was taken to detailed concept and renders.
