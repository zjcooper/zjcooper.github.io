Push it, fork it or crane it, and catch any spill on the way.

Produced at my day job with an industrial fuel-equipment manufacturer. Shown with permission.

The brief

A three-vessel high-flow bag filter set needed to move around an industrial site. It had to be pushed by hand for short moves, lifted by forklift or crane for longer ones, and catch spills during filter changes.

What I did

  • Designed a welded mild-steel chassis with 200 × 100 and 100 × 50 RHS mains, 50 × 50 SHS cross-members, forklift pockets at 900 mm centres, four lifting eyes and 100 mm castors.
  • Designed an 81 L bund tray in 5 mm 304 stainless, folded from a single 1549 × 949 mm laser-cut blank, with a DN25 drain valve.
  • Drew a bent-pipe handle, with the bend geometry marked as reference so the tube bender has room to work.
  • Documented the loaded arrangement with the vessels, valves, camlocks, gauges and test points.
  • Issued a six-sheet A3 pack for construction, plus a three-sheet general arrangement.

The result

The fabricator built the trolley from the drawings alone. A later revision changed the filter layout, lowered the piping, added crane points and lengthened the trolley, and it ran through the same pack.
