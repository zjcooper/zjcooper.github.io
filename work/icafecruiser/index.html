iCafeCruiser electric motorbike

An urban electric café racer concept, sized from real rider data and built around a city commute.

Project
University project
Year
2023
My role
Solo. Concept, ergonomics, CAD and renders
Tools
3D CAD, rendering, anthropometric data

A café racer for the city, sized for small and tall riders.

The brief

Design an electric motorbike for short trips in a dense city, branded as a BMW concept for a university studio brief. It had to look good, park easily and carry a bag.

What I did

  • Combined a classic café racer shape with an electric drivetrain, aluminium and plastic body panels, and 17-inch chrome rims.
  • Sized it from anthropometric data. The seat height comes from 5th percentile female buttock height, and the grip reach spans 5th percentile female to 95th percentile male.
  • Made the seat adjustable by 10 cm either way to cover that range.
  • Mapped a rider's day across a 3.2 km commute, including parking on footpaths and mounting kerbs.
  • Designed a waterproof storage compartment that locks with the bike's power, a covered charging port and a rotating digital dash.
  • Sketched a companion app for display and seat profiles, ride summaries, and remote battery and tyre-pressure checks.

The result

A fully rendered concept with a 136 cm wheelbase, 22 cm ground clearance, a 75 cm seat and a 160 km target range. It's a design concept only, with no prototype.
