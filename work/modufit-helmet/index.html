Modufit Light helmet

An open-face helmet for city riders, with clip-on attachments and padding fitted from a head scan.

Project
University project
Year
2023
My role
Solo. Research, form models, CAD and renders
Tools
3D scanning, clay modelling, Fusion 360, Meshmixer

A lighter helmet for bikes, e-scooters and skateboards, shaped on a scan of a real head.

The brief

An advanced manufacturing course asked for a product that could be mass customised. I chose helmets for people riding bikes, e-scooters and skateboards around the city. Most helmets I looked at were bulky, heavy and uncomfortable, and few suited an everyday city look.

What I did

  • Mapped existing helmets by how busy they look against how much protection they give. The gap was a simple-looking helmet with strong protection.
  • 3D scanned my own head and printed it. Then I shaped clay form models directly on the print.
  • Modelled the shell in Fusion 360 and refined the mesh in Meshmixer.
  • Kept the face open for vision, airflow and talking to people. A slim aluminium chin bar protects the face.
  • Designed 3D-printed TPU lattice padding, sized from each rider's own head scan.
  • Added three clip-on attachments: a mouthguard, a tinted visor and headphones. Hard rubber push buttons with aluminium cores hold them in.
  • Planned the materials and processes. The shell is injection-moulded ABS, the visor is laser-cut and heat-formed polycarbonate, and the chin bar is extruded and bent aluminium.

The result

A rendered concept with a full material and process plan. Riders can change the look and the level of protection without buying a new helmet. They can also replace a worn part on its own.
