Bluetooth speaker teardown

A consumer speaker taken apart in stages and rebuilt in CAD, one component at a time.

Rebuilding a product from the inside out, one part at a time.

The brief

Take a real consumer Bluetooth speaker apart and rebuild it digitally. The model had to capture the internal structure, not only the outer shell.

What I did

  • Took the speaker apart in stages, photographing each step: grilles, panels, screws and internals.
  • Measured and modelled each component as its own part: the frame, the front and rear panels, the grilles, the battery and board package, and the control panel.
  • Assembled the parts in the same order the real product goes together.

The result

A component-level digital copy of a production product, with a photo record that traces every part back to the real thing.
