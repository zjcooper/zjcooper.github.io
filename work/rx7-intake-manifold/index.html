RX7 rotary intake manifold

A one-off intake manifold with a lost mould, captured and rebuilt as a clean CAD model.

Project
Freelance job
Year
2025
My role
Capture, CAD rebuild and flow check
Tools
SolidWorks, SolidWorks Flow Simulation, 3D scanning, silicone moulding

One part left, no mould, and passages you can't measure from the outside.

The brief

A mechanic had a custom intake manifold for an RX7 rotary engine. It was a one-off, and the mould it came from had gone missing. He had one manifold left and no way to make another. The internal passages couldn't be measured from outside without cutting the part open.

What I did

  • Took a silicone mould of the internal passages. That gave a clean negative of the inside without damaging the original.
  • 3D scanned the silicone mould and the outside of the manifold.
  • Cleaned up the scan data, patching holes and smoothing the surfaces.
  • Rebuilt the part in SolidWorks as a parametric model, split into the internal passages, the outer housing and the ports. It took five rounds of refinement.
  • Ran a CFD flow study to check the runners before anything was committed to manufacture.

The result

A CAD model built for manufacture, with files ready for casting, machining or 3D printing. The owner can reproduce the manifold without cutting new tooling from scratch or risking the original.
