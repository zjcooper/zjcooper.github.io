One model, three outputs. The build, the drawings and the sales renders.

Produced at my day job with an industrial fuel-equipment manufacturer. Shown with permission.

The brief

A self-bunded fuel cube is a double-walled tank. The outer shell forms a bund that holds the full fuel volume if the inner tank ever leaks. The product needed a complete CAD package: a model the workshop could build from, drawings, and renders for sales.

What I did

  • Modelled the cube as a weldment and sheet-metal assembly: the inner tank, the outer bund skin, lifting and forklift points, a lockable equipment cabinet, and the fill, dispense and access points.
  • Produced the fabrication drawings for the workshop from the same model.
  • Rendered a set of ten photoreal images in KeyShot, in the studio and on site.

The result

One model drove the build, the drawings and the marketing images. Because they all come from the same source, the renders show exactly what the workshop builds.
