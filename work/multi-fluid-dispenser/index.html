A water-tight stainless housing that still opens up for service.

Produced at my day job with an industrial fuel-equipment manufacturer. Shown with permission.

The brief

A custom multi-fluid fuel dispenser for a mine site needed a housing. It had to wrap an internal pipework manifold of valves, meters and filters, keep water out, and still let a technician in for service.

What I did

  • Designed a riveted skeleton of 3 mm stainless mounts, wrapped in folded 1.2 mm stainless skin panels: front, hinged door, back, sides, cap and rain lid.
  • Drew every part for laser cutting and folding, with flat patterns and bend lines.
  • Used blind rivets for the structure and M6 nutserts for the panels that come off for service.
  • Issued an 11-sheet AS 1100 fabrication pack that went straight to the laser cutter and press brake.

The result

The workshop fabricated the enclosure from the pack, and the dispenser was delivered to site. The renders show the stainless housing on its own.
