Medical laser thermometer

An off-the-shelf thermometer torn down and redesigned around its real internals, split for moulding.

Project
University project
Year
2024
My role
Solo. Teardown, CAD, drawings and renders
Tools
SolidWorks, KeyShot

A softer, medical shape, built around the parts that were already inside.

The brief

Take a real laser thermometer apart and design a new housing around its existing internals. The new form had to move away from the aggressive "gun" look toward something softer and more medical. It also had to be possible to manufacture.

What I did

  • Stripped the thermometer completely, then photographed and measured every internal part: the circuit board, LCD, laser module, batteries and trigger switch.
  • Surfaced the new housing in SolidWorks with lofts and boundary surfaces, and split it into three mouldable bodies.
  • Added ribs to hold the board and laser, a boss that lines the trigger up with the real switch, snap-locks for the battery cover and tip cap, and screw bosses to join the shells.
  • Documented it in a six-sheet A3 drawing pack with a bill of materials.
  • Rendered the final design in KeyShot, including the internals and exploded views.

The result

A redesign that fits the original electronics and is split for injection moulding. It covers the whole loop, from teardown to a documented design ready for tooling.
