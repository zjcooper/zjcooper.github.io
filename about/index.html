About

Hi, I'm Zac.

I'm a mechanical draftsman and industrial designer based in Brisbane. By day I produce CAD models and fabrication drawings for an industrial fuel-equipment manufacturer. On the side I take on freelance drafting and design jobs, mostly remotely.

I studied industrial design at QUT, and I'm now studying mechanical engineering there. In 2025 I spent two months at an aerospace design firm in Spain. I worked on CAD, rapid prototyping and composite mould design.

Before all that I worked in fencing, so I'm used to measuring on site and making things fit the real world. That's still how I work. I measure carefully, model it properly, and hand over something you can build from or bolt on.
