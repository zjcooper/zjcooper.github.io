Services

What I can do for you

I do most of my work remotely, from photos, sketches, old drawings or 3D files. I work with businesses that need drafting done, and with people who need a part designed or made. When a job needs measuring in person, I can visit around Brisbane.

Remote

Fabrication drawings

Workshop-ready drawing packs to AS 1100. General arrangements, part drawings, flat patterns for laser cutting and folding, BOMs and revisions.

You get: a PDF drawing pack, plus DXF and STEP files for the workshop.

Example: Hi-flow filter trolley, Multi-fluid dispenser enclosure

Remote

CAD conversion and laser files

Hand sketches, PDFs, old paper drawings and scans turned into clean CAD. DXF files ready for laser cutting, plasma or CNC.

You get: DWG, DXF or STEP files, checked and ready to cut or edit.

Example: Multi-fluid dispenser enclosure

Remote

CAD support for businesses

Extra SolidWorks and drafting capacity for fabricators and engineering firms. By the job or by the hour, working to your templates and standards.

You get: models and drawings in your format, ready to issue.

Example: Hi-flow filter trolley, E-scooter front wheel hub

Remote

Product design and renders

From a first sketch to a model you can manufacture. Renders you can use to pitch, sell or check the look before tooling.

You get: a 3D model, renders and a parts list.

Example: Rapimod K9 harness, Self-bunded fuel cube

Remote

Design checks and calculations

Mounting geometry, strut and spring sizing, fits and tolerances. Worked from first principles and written up so you can follow the numbers.

You get: a short report with the method, the numbers and a recommendation.

Example: Gas strut placement for a heavy lid, E-scooter front wheel hub

Remote or on site

Reverse engineering

Old, broken or discontinued parts rebuilt as clean CAD. I work from the part itself, careful measurements or a 3D scan.

You get: STEP, STL or DXF files and a drawing, plus a printed test part if you want one.

Example: RX7 rotary intake manifold, Medical laser thermometer

On site, Brisbane

Custom parts and fit-outs

Replacement windows, panels, covers and brackets. I measure on site and cut or print the part to fit, in acrylic and other sheet plastics or in 3D-printed plastics.

You get: a finished part, fitted or ready to fit.

Example: Caravan window replacement, Air-con window panel

Scope

What I don't take on

Keeping to what I do well means you get a better job. For these, I'll point you to the right person.

Electrical work

I don't do electrical wiring of any kind, including 12 V, or electrical drawings.

Certified structural sign-off

Load-rated designs and certification need a registered engineer (RPEQ). I'll refer you.

Heavy fabrication and welding

I produce the drawings, and a fabricator builds the steelwork.

Pricing

How pricing works

Every job is different, so I quote after I've seen photos or the part itself. Small jobs usually get a fixed price.

Remote drafting and modelling can be quoted by the job or by the hour.

FAQ

Common questions

Can you work from photos?

Yes. Photos work best with a tape measure or ruler in the shot, plus any sizes you already know. If the part has to be exact, I can measure it in person around Brisbane or 3D scan it.

What files will I get?

Usually a PDF drawing pack, DWG or DXF files for cutting, and STEP files of the 3D model. I can also supply STL files for printing and native SolidWorks files.

Do you work outside Brisbane?

Yes, for remote work. Drafting, CAD and design jobs run by email, so it doesn't matter where you are. Site visits and fitting are around Brisbane.

How long does a job take?

It depends on the job. Tell me when you need it by, and I'll say in the quote whether I can meet that date.

Can you work to our templates and standards?

Yes. I can draw in your title block and to your drafting standards, and hand over files that are ready to issue.

Can you make the part as well?

For small parts, yes. I can laser cut acrylic and other non-metal sheet, or 3D print in plastics. For steel and larger parts, I prepare the drawings and files for your fabricator.

Can you certify a design?

No. I do design and drafting only. Load-rated designs and certification need a registered engineer (RPEQ), and I'll refer you to one.

Got a sketch, a part or a drawing to sort out?

Send it over with a few photos or sizes. I'll tell you what's possible and what it will cost.
