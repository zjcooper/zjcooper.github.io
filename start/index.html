Tell me about the job

Send a sketch, photos or drawings and a short description. Most jobs can be done remotely. I'll reply with questions or a quote.

What helps me quote

  1. A sketch, an old drawing, a PDF or a 3D file, if you have one.
  2. Photos of the part or the space, with a tape measure or ruler in the shot.
  3. Any sizes you already know, and what the part does.
  4. What you want at the end. That might be files, drawings, a finished part or a fitted part.
  5. When you need it by.

What happens next

  1. I read your enquiry and reply by email.
  2. If I need to see it in person, we book a time to measure.
  3. You get a quote. Work starts once you accept it.

Put this in your email

  • Your name and suburb
  • What you need, for example drawings, a sketch converted to CAD, laser files or a part made
  • What the job is, with any sizes you know
  • When you need it by, and a rough budget if you have one
  • Photos, sketches, PDFs or 3D files as attachments

I only use your details to reply to your enquiry and quote the job. I don't share them or add you to a mailing list.
