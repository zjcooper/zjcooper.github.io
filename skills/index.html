Skills and equipment

I turn sketches, parts and photos into drawings, 3D models and finished parts. Most of the work runs from my desk, so I can do most jobs remotely.

Drafting and documentation

Workshop drawings that a fabricator can quote and build from.

  • AS 1100 drawing packs
  • General arrangements
  • Part drawings
  • Flat patterns for laser cutting and folding
  • Bills of materials
  • Revision control
  • P&IDs for fluid systems
  • PDF and scan to CAD conversion
  • Measured floor plans of existing spaces (not for building approval)

Seen in: Hi-flow filter trolley, Multi-fluid dispenser enclosure, E-scooter front wheel hub

3D CAD

Parametric models that update cleanly when the design changes.

  • SolidWorks parts and assemblies
  • Sheet metal
  • Weldments
  • Configurations and design tables
  • Surface modelling
  • Fusion 360 for freeform shapes

Seen in: Self-bunded fuel cube, RX7 rotary intake manifold, Modufit Light helmet

Reverse engineering

Existing parts measured or scanned, then rebuilt as clean CAD.

  • 3D LiDAR scanning
  • Mesh clean-up
  • Scan-to-CAD rebuilds
  • Replacement parts
  • Teardowns
  • Measuring on site

Seen in: RX7 rotary intake manifold, Medical laser thermometer, Bluetooth speaker teardown

Product and industrial design

Checks and calculations

Numbers worked from first principles and written up so you can follow them.

  • Hand calculations
  • Mounting and strut geometry
  • Fits and tolerances
  • Flow checks in SolidWorks Flow Simulation
  • MATLAB

These checks are for design decisions. Certified structural sign-off goes to an RPEQ.

Seen in: Gas strut placement for a heavy lid, E-scooter front wheel hub, RX7 rotary intake manifold

Making and fitting

  • Measuring and fitting on site

Seen in: Caravan window replacement, Air-con window panel, The Yard pegboard seat
