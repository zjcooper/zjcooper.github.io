How a job runs

From a sketch or a photo to drawings you can build from

Most jobs run remotely from start to finish. I only need to visit when a part or a space has to be measured in person.

01

Send it over

Photos with sizes, a sketch, an old drawing, a PDF or a 3D file. I'll ask for anything that's missing.

02

Model

I build it in SolidWorks and check the fit on screen before anything is made.

03

Draw

You get drawings to AS 1100, plus STEP and DXF files a workshop can cut and build from.

04

Make

If you want it made, I can laser cut or 3D print small parts, or you take the pack to your fabricator.
